# Code of conduct

AssetNest welcomes contributors and users of all experience levels and backgrounds.
This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews, and other repository interactions.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and considerate when asking questions or giving feedback.
- Discuss the work and its impact; avoid personal attacks.
- Make space for different perspectives and levels of experience.
- Accept constructive feedback and correct mistakes in good faith.
- Respect privacy and use fictional data in examples, logs, and screenshots.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized content, doxxing, spam, and sustained disruption
are not welcome. Do not publish someone else's private information without permission.

## Reporting concerns

For repository conduct concerns, contact the maintainer through the public contact listed on
[the maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/MohamedRF). Include links and enough context to
understand what happened; do not repeat private information in public issues.

You can also use GitHub's **Report abuse** controls for behavior that violates GitHub's policies.
For software vulnerabilities, use the separate [security reporting process](SECURITY.md).

## Moderation

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock conversations, or restrict participation
when needed to keep the project respectful and productive. Decisions should consider the context,
severity, and repeated nature of the behavior. Apply this policy consistently and respect the
privacy of people involved.
